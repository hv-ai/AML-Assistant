What you can investigate

Typology explanations, source-supported warning indicators, legitimate explanations and qualifications, and evidence an analyst may request. Use the seven topic buttons in the app, then inspect supporting passages or ask a more specific follow-up question.

How evidence is handled

Original public files and their checksums are saved locally. PDF citations identify physical PDF pages; extract line numbers are local locators. The model selects evidence IDs and the app inserts the original text. Unknown IDs are rejected, and some observable overclaims are omitted. These checks do not establish that every paraphrase is fully supported.

Customer controls: updated source

FinCEN consolidated CDD FAQs, updated May 6, 2026, are now indexed with physical PDF page locators. Individual answers retain their issue/update dates. FAQs clarify requirements but are not the binding CFR text or the relief order itself. The 2014 KYC testimony is historical background, excluded from ordinary control-question routing.

Coverage gaps beyond the shortcuts

Further targeted source and answer validation is needed for professional laundering networks, underground banking/hawala, bulk cash smuggling, real estate, gambling, and laundering proceeds of fraud/cybercrime, drugs and environmental crime. A mention in a broad national assessment does not establish dedicated coverage. Terrorist financing and sanctions evasion are related but distinct risk domains.

Scope and remaining work

US banking is the main research scope; FATF/Egmont material is international context. Advisories and risk assessments are distinguished from regulations. Historical publications are dated and are not treated as verified current-law determinations. News briefs remain separate from the question-answer library.

The 2026 Treasury assessment includes other threats and channels, but coverage outside these seven areas still needs targeted passage and answer review. A red flag is an investigative signal, not proof of wrongdoing. This app does not make customer, SAR-filing or compliance determinations.
